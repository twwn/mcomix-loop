- **Quality of a scaling filter is measured with sine stripes, not by eye.** Stripes of contrast 100 (127.5 + 100 sin), scaled to 42% of their width: a period of 12 source pixels is below the result's Nyquist limit and should keep its contrast (sharpness); a period of 3.3 is above it and should come out flat (moiré). Half the range of a middle row, past 8 pixels of edge, is the number. STATE/probes/scale_moire.py prints it for gdk-pixbuf's TILES/BILINEAR/HYPER and Pillow's BICUBIC/LANCZOS: kept 92.5/92.5/91.5/98.0/101.0, moiré 33.5/33.5/27.5/10.0/0.0 (at c2579869). gdk-pixbuf 2.44's HYPER differs from BILINEAR (not an alias), TILES equals BILINEAR when reducing.
- **Pillow chooses which pass runs first by the whole input's height** (Resample.c, ImagingResampleInner: vertical first when `imIn->ysize - ysize > (imIn->xsize - xsize) * 2`). A band of rows scaled with `box=` into the whole picture therefore runs the vertical pass first where the whole page ran the horizontal one, and on line art (all 0 and 255, so the intermediate clips Lanczos' ringing) differs by up to 35 levels in a third of the pixels. Both orders are equally far from a float reference (`convert('F').resize(...)`), so neither is wrong, but bands must agree with each other and with the unbanded result: crop the band's rows plus the kernel's margin (`ceil(support * max(1, ratio)) + 1` source rows each side), then resize the crop with a box relative to it. That matches the whole page within one level (0e87b4b8). A `box=` hair inside the whole picture (`H - 1e-9`) is the unboxed path exactly; the float32 parsing of the box is not the cause.
- **Pillow's reducing_gap applied per band misaligns the blocks**: reduce once, before banding, with the box then `(0, 0, W / fx, H / fy)` as Pillow's own resize does it.
- **STATE/probes/scale_fitcheck.py** compares fit_in_rectangle() under Lanczos and bicubic with Pillow's own one-piece resize (reducing_gap=3) on line art, RGB and RGBA, and times it: at 0e87b4b8 every max diff was 0 or 1; 2400x3400 to 1008x1428 26 ms (71 before banding), 6000x9000 to 800x1200 92 ms (the reduce and the pixbuf/Pillow conversions are single-threaded). Run with `cd REPO && timeout -k 5 60 python3 <probe>`.
- **The GPU already scales textures down from mipmaps.** A plain texture node (Gdk.Texture.snapshot) reduced to 42% gave the same moiré as `append_scaled_texture(..., Gsk.ScalingFilter.TRILINEAR)` on cairo (33.5), GL (44.5) and Vulkan (42.0); LINEAR gave 95.5. So an animation's frames need no filter of MComix's choosing (rejected.md). STATE/probes/gsk_filter_moire.py measures it per renderer under xvfb-run (it prints MESA DRI3 warnings; GL and Vulkan still render, through llvmpipe).
- **A page turn with a scaling quality** is timed by STATE/probes/test_zz_page_turn.py with `SQ=<value>` set after the book loads (insert `if os.environ.get("SQ"): prefs["scaling quality"] = int(os.environ["SQ"])` before the OPTS handling; the saved probe lacks it). At 0e87b4b8, big60.cbz in 1280x900: bilinear 6.6 ms median, Lanczos 10.4 (18.4 before banding), double page 12.1 and 23.0 (32.9).
- (at 75bb4e1a) **Thumbnails are measured the same way**, at their box: stripes on a 2400x3400 page saved as PNG and as JPEG (quality 95), load_pixbuf_size(path, 160, 160), half the range of the middle row past 3 pixels of edge, against Pillow's Lanczos of the whole page. Period 30 (1.4 / scale) is too fine for the thumbnail; 15 and 10 probe what the loaders' first step (gdk-pixbuf at_size, Pillow's draft) aliases before the final fit, which no final filter can take back. Timing is STATE/probes/bench_thumb_decode.py (box 128, mean of ten).
