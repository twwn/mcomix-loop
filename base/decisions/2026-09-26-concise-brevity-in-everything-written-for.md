"Write down to use that concise brevity in style from now on" - release notes, `docs/`, README, issue and PR templates, SECURITY and CONTRIBUTING, workflow summaries and the reports: short lines, one fact per line or sentence, no compound sentences stringing changes together. More entries are fine; fewer words per entry is the point. Commit messages keep cause and evidence, said plainly (7fe93b58 is the model).
